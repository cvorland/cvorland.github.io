2022 · Commentary · PloS one

Accounting for the clustering and nesting effects verifies most conclusions. Corrected analysis of: "Randomized nutrient bar supplementation improves exercise-associated changes in plasma metabolome in adolescents and adult family members at cardiometabolic risk"

Yasaman Jamshidi-Naeini; Lilian Golzarri-Arroyo; Colby J Vorland; Andrew W Brown; Stephanie Dickinson; David B Allison

Reanalyzes a nutrient-bar trial with models that account for household clustering and nesting, finding that most conclusions remain unchanged.
