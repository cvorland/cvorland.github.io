Fetal sex effects on maternal health can now be tested via randomization: A first-in-class illustration in cows on glucoregulatory outcomes

Aridany Suarez-Trujillo; Colby J Vorland; Griffin T Nicholls; Daniella E Chusyd; Chelsie Parker; Lilian Golzarri-Arroyo; Sophia Swann; Bethany J Funnell; Kara R Stewart; David B Allison

Demonstrates randomized assignment of fetal sex in dairy cows as a way to test causal effects on maternal glucose regulation.
