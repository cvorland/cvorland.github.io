Acute High Dietary Phosphorus Following Low-Phosphorus Diet Acclimation Does Not Enhance Intestinal Fractional Phosphorus Absorption in Nephrectomized Male Rats

Kendal M Burstad; Dennis P Cladis; Colby J Vorland; Meryl E Wastney; Annabel Biruete; James M Dominguez 2nd; Kalisha D O'Neill; Neal X Chen; Sharon M Moe; Kathleen M Hill Gallant

Tests whether a high-phosphorus meal after a low-phosphorus diet increases intestinal absorption in male rats with reduced kidney function, finding no enhanced absorption efficiency.
