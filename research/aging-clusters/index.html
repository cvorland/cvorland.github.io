Improving statistical rigor in animal aging research by addressing clustering and nesting effects: Illustration with the National Institute on Aging's Intervention Testing Program data

Erik S Parker; Lilian Golzarri-Arroyo; Stephanie Dickinson; Beate Henschel; Luis-Enrique Becerra-Garcia; Thirupathi R Mokalla; Olivia C Robertson; Deependra K Thapa; Colby J Vorland; David B Allison

Uses mouse lifespan data from the Interventions Testing Program to show how accounting for cage-level clustering and nesting can change statistical conclusions.
