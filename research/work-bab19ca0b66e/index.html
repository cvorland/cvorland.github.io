Effect of dietary phosphorus intake and age on intestinal phosphorus absorption efficiency and phosphorus balance in male rats

Colby J Vorland; Pamela J Lachcik; Loretta O Aromeh; Sharon M Moe; Neal X Chen; Kathleen M Hill Gallant

Examines phosphorus absorption and balance across ages and dietary phosphorus levels in male rats, finding higher absorption in younger rats but no dietary-level effect on the measured intestinal absorption.
