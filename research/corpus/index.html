SPIRIT-CONSORT-TM: a corpus for assessing transparency of clinical trial protocol and results publications

Lan Jiang; Colby J Vorland; Xiangji Ying; Andrew W Brown; Joe D Menke; Gibong Hong; Mengfei Lan; Evan Mayo-Wilson; Halil Kilicoglu

Introduces an expert-annotated corpus of 200 trial publications and language-processing models for recognizing SPIRIT and CONSORT reporting items.
