Determination of sex differences requires formal test for differences: Comment on "lipoprotein-subclass particle numbers in children with abdominal obesity"

Colby J Vorland; Lilian Golzarri-Arroyo; Donna L Maney; Andrew W Brown

Explains that claims of sex differences in lipoprotein measures among children with abdominal obesity require a formal statistical test of the difference.
