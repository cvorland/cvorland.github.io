From Model Organisms to Humans, the Opportunity for More Rigor in Methodologic and Statistical Analysis, Design, and Interpretation of Aging and Senescence Research

Daniella E Chusyd; Steven N Austad; Andrew W Brown; Xiwei Chen; Stephanie L Dickinson; Keisuke Ejima; David Fluharty; Lilian Golzarri-Arroyo; Richard Holden; Yasaman Jamshidi-Naeini; Doug Landsittel; Stella Lartey; Edward Mannix; Colby J Vorland; David B Allison

Reviews recurring design, statistical, and interpretation problems in aging research and offers recommendations for more rigorous studies.
