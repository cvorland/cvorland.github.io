Text classification models for assessing the completeness of randomized controlled trial publications based on CONSORT reporting guidelines

Lan Jiang; Mengfei Lan; Joe D Menke; Colby J Vorland; Halil Kilicoglu

Develops and compares text-classification approaches for identifying whether randomized-trial publications report individual CONSORT checklist items.
