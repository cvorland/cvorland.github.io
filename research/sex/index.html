Claims of sex-dependent effects in the behavioral and brain sciences are infrequently supported by valid statistical comparisons

Madeline T Olivier; Andrew W Brown; Simon Chung; Colby J Vorland; Donna L Maney

In a sample of 200 behavioral and brain science papers claiming sex-dependent effects, 24% supplied appropriate statistical evidence supporting the claim.
