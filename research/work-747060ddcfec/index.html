Colby J Vorland; Paola P Mattey-Mora; Luis M Mestre; Xiwei Chen; Stephanie L Dickinson; Andrew W Brown; David B Allison

Identifies effect-size calculation and reporting problems that limit reproducibility of a review of blueberries and cognitive performance during aging.
