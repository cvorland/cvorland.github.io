Questions on 'Intervention effects of a kindergarten-based health promotion programme on obesity related behavioural outcomes and BMI percentiles'

Colby J Vorland; Andrew W Brown; Chanaka N Kahathuduwa; John A Dawson; Nana Gletsu-Miller; Theodore K Kyle; Lehana Thabane; David B Allison

Raises questions about a kindergarten health-promotion study and its reported effects on obesity-related behaviors and body-mass-index percentiles.
