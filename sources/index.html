Sources & catalog notes

A connected catalog of publications and open research materials.

The source collection was reviewed on 11 September 2026. The catalog combines 152 bibliographic entries into 90 research works, including 81 papers and preprints. Published versions, preprints, and directly associated materials share one record, with their individual metadata preserved.

Bibliographic sources

  • All 91 entries on Google Scholar were reconciled with the catalog.
  • 77 records from PubMed, including author lists, publication details, identifiers, and available indexing terms.
  • OpenAlex supplemented these records with repository materials, related versions, and other outputs. Candidate records from fragmented author profiles were checked against titles, author lists, and other sources.
  • The CV dated 22 February 2026 contributed 77 numbered publication entries. Duplicate listings were consolidated. Publisher pages, repositories, and full texts resolved gaps and discrepancies.

Reading the map

Each work has one home island and can connect to other islands. Island areas reflect primary papers and preprints; connected-paper counts are shown separately. Solid buildings mark primary works, and outlined buildings mark secondary connections. Hovering or selecting a work reveals its routes between islands. Cities group work by subtheme. Ships show work in progress: standalone conference abstracts and posters, plus manually selected work. Each ship sits beside its home island and has no duplicate houses. Abstracts already associated with a published paper are included within that paper’s record. Featured work is an editorial starting point.

Open-science icons

Icons link to verified preprints, shared data, shared code, registrations, protocols, and open-access text. Hover or focus on an icon for its label; the full record lists each linked resource. Data may consist of extracted evidence, audit results, or derived analysis data rather than participant-level observations.

Data and code badges require evidence of accessible files. A generic project link or a statement that files are available on request is insufficient. An absent icon means a resource was not verified in this review, rather than that it does not exist. Repository contents and access can change.

Versions, authors, and classification

Preprints and directly associated materials are combined with their published work when the relationship is supported. Corrections to an author's own article appear within that record, while independent commentary and distinct research studies retain separate records. Each component preserves its own citation and ordered author list; initials remain where the source does not supply a reliable full name. Repository creators and contributors can have different roles, which are retained in the metadata.

Research themes and brief summaries are editorial classifications informed by titles, abstracts, the supplied CV, and research statements. They are not labels assigned by the indexing services. Citation counts, where supplied in the metadata, are source-specific snapshots.
