Traffic Light Diets for Childhood Obesity: Disambiguation of Terms and Critical Review of Application, Food Categorization, and Strength of Evidence

Colby J Vorland; Michelle M Bohan Brown; Michelle I Cardel; Andrew W Brown

Distinguishes traffic-light food labels from the wider childhood-obesity programs that use them and finds insufficient evidence to isolate the labels’ causal contribution.
