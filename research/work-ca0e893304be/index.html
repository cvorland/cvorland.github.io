SPIRIT-CONSORT-ELM: Element-Level Annotated Dataset and Large Language Model Approach for Assessing Randomized Controlled Trial Reporting

Lan Jiang; Xiangji Ying; Andrew W Brown; Mengfei Lan; Wenxuan Song; Joe D Menke; Colby J Vorland; Evan Mayo-Wilson; Halil Kilicoglu

Develops detailed reporting annotations and an evidence-retrieval plus language-model pipeline to assess whether trial protocols and results report individual SPIRIT and CONSORT elements.

Preprint; this version has not been peer reviewed.
