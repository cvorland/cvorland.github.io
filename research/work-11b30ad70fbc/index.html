Improving open and rigorous science: ten key future research opportunities related to rigor, reproducibility, and transparency in scientific research

Danny Valdez; Colby J Vorland; Andrew W Brown; Evan Mayo-Wilson; Justin Otten; Richard Ball; Sean Grant; Rachel Levy; Dubravka Svetina Valdivia; David B Allison

Reports ten research priorities from a workshop on improving rigor, reproducibility, and transparency, including statistical errors, reporting, education, and automated assistance.
