Effects of Excessive Dietary Phosphorus Intake on Bone Health

Colby J Vorland; Elizabeth R Stremke; Ranjani N Moorthi; Kathleen M Hill Gallant

Reviews dietary phosphorus sources, absorption, and the evidence linking excessive phosphorus intake, particularly additives, to bone and mineral metabolism.
